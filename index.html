How it works

It knows you're walking

Steps are counted from the motion sensor, and you count as reading when the phone is held up in portrait with Lane on screen.

It locks your line

After about two seconds, the way you're heading becomes the line. Drift past your sensitivity angle and it nudges you back.

One long buzz · steer leftTwo short · steer right
It knows a corner from a drift

A sharp turn, or staying off line for eight seconds, sets a new line. Stop or lower the phone and it resets.
