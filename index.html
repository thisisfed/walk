How it works

Steps are counted from the accelerometer. You count as "looking" when the phone is held up in portrait, tilted towards your face, with this page on screen.

After about 2 seconds of walking and looking, your current direction becomes the line. If you drift past the sensitivity angle, it alerts you: one long buzz = steer left, two short buzzes = steer right.

A sharp turn (over 55°), or staying off-line for 8 seconds, counts as a deliberate turn and a new line is set. Stopping or lowering the phone resets it.

Vibration works in Chrome on Android. iPhones don't let web pages vibrate, so use the beep there.
